If you already own them

Default is to stop adding, not to sell. A hard exit needs two weekly closes below the 38-week line AND a fall of 30% or more from the peak.

Weekly routine

  1. Check the light. QQQ above its 150-day average: invest the full weekly amount. Below: invest half, hold the rest in cash/T-bills.
  2. Buy the ticket. New money only into BUY and STARTER names at the weights shown.
  3. Cap every name at 25% of the stock sleeve.
  4. Treat highly correlated names as one bet (e.g. FIX, KLAC, NVDA). Keep the cluster under about 40%.
  5. Exit check. One weekly close below the 38-week line: stop adding. Two in a row: review. Two in a row plus 30% off peak: sell.
  6. Re-entry is automatic. A name becomes BUY again when it closes a week above its 38-week line and is ahead of SPY over 12 months.
  7. Hold winners. No profit-taking on a BUY name.
